LondonIsForLovers

★★★★★

From the diary of Doreen Lester (I took the liberty to correct the spelling errors):

Dear Diary!
What a day it has been! For the first time since that big bomb went off and we had to run away from home, Mummy and me finally slept in a real bed again! A nice man took us in and we can live in his bookshop! It's closed, but there are so many books! He said, he'll read some to me because I'm so brave!

I was trying to choose one when another man came with a suitcase. I thought he also didn't have a home anymore, but he was really smartly dressed with a hat and a suit and a tie. He hid something from his suitcase and whispered a couple of words to Mr. Fell before he loudly thanked him for “the books” while side-eyeing me (he never even got any books!!!) and then he drove off in a really fancy looking car.

Later at night, after Mr. Fell read me Peter Pan, I sneaked into the shop and looked for what the mysterious man had left there. But it was just some papers with symbols and letters that didn't make words.

It was weird. But I went to sleep and had the best sleep I ever had! (And Mummy said so, too. Even though there were more bombs during the night. But we didn't hear them at all!)

Mr. Fell said not to worry about a thing, and then he read me a story about a bear that is the teddy bear of a boy, but also a real bear. And after that, he made a farthing disappear in his hand and appear in my ear!

I think maybe Mr. Fell is a character from a book that came to life like the teddy bear.
